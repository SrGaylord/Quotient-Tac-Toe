How to Play Quotient Tac Toe

1

Roll the Dice: On your turn, roll two 6-sided dice. Multiply them together to find your Target Dividend.

2

Calculate Quotient: Enter a valid divisor to calculate a quotient ($Q$) that appears on the 3x3 board grid.

3

Claim the Tile: Tap the corresponding quotient square on the board to claim it for your team!

4

Stealing Squares: You can steal squares owned by your opponent if your rolled dividend can produce that square's quotient!

5

Win Condition: Connect 3 squares in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to win!
